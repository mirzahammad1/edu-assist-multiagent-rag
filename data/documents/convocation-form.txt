23RD CONVOCATION : APRIL 20, 2026
KARACHI INSTITUTE OF ECONOMICS AND TECHNOLOGY (KIET)
Dear Student
1. You are a prospective candidate for the award of degree. We are pleased to inform you
that the 23rd Convocation of Karachi Institute of Economics and Technology (KIET) is planned
at 06:00 pm on Monday, April 20, 2026 at EXPO CENTER (Hall #: 5 & 6), Karachi.
2. We are enclosing the Convocation Form for admission to the convocation. Please return
the form by Sunday, April 05, 2026. Kindly note that you will be included in the list of
graduates, only if you fulfill all the degree requirements till Summer-2025. The students who
are out of city can send their Convocation Form through email to their respective campus via
the following email addresses:
Main Campus: muhammad.kashif@kiet.edu.pk
City Campus (Shahrah-e-Faisal Site): zulfiqar.ali@kiet.edu.pk
City Campus (North Nazimabad Site): satif@kiet.edu.pk
3. An amount of Rs. 20,000/- will be charged to cover expenditures of the Convocation.
Therefore, kindly send us a pay order / bank draft in the name of KIET along with the enclosed
application form or alternatively, you may adjust your security deposit/caution money in
Convocation charges and pay the Pay order of the remaining amount accordingly. The last
date to pay the amount for the convocation is April 05, 2026.
4. Please bring original CNIC on rehearsal day. Due to security reasons, entry will not be
permitted without invitation card and original CNIC on Convocation day. Please be assured
that only parents or spouse from your family will be allowed to participate in the Convocation.
5. Kindly attend the rehearsal planned at 2:30 pm on April 19, 2026 at EXPO CENTER
(Hall #: 5 & 6), Karachi. Needless to say that rehearsal is absolutely necessary for a smooth
and graceful ceremony. If you fail to attend the rehearsal, you will not be allowed to
participate in the Convocation-2026.
6. It is pertinent to mention that No reservation/ query regarding Gold/ Silver Medals will be
entertained after April 06, 2026.
7. The robes will be provided by the supplier at EXPO CENTER (Hall #: 5 & 6), Karachi
before the rehearsal on April 19, 2026 upon paying a deposit of Rs. 3,200/-. Out of which
Rs. 2,500/- will be refunded upon returning the robe after the convocation. Please bring the
exact cash amount as ATM facility will not be available.
Important Schedule
Rehearsal Day Sunday, April 19, 2026 Student’s arrival time : 02:30 pm
Convocation Day Monday, April 20, 2026 Students arrival time : 02:30 pm
Best Wishes,
Registrar – KIET
