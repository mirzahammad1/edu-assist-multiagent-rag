BS Business Analytics and Enterprise Systems
BS Business Analytics and Enterprise Systems is an undergraduate program designed to equip students with a strong foundation in data analysis, business intelligence, and modern enterprise technologies. The program integrates core computing concepts with business management principles, enabling graduates to analyze large datasets, support strategic decision-making, and implement enterprise-level information systems. Students develop skills in analytics tools, databases, programming, and organizational processes, preparing them for careers in data analytics, enterprise solutions, IT consultancy, and digital business transformation across various industries.
