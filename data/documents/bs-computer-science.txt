BS Computer Science
Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BS-CS)
The BS Computer Science program provides a balanced mixture of theory and practical experiences at foundation and advance levels of Computer Science and Information System discipline. It covers all new dimensions of information systems from Internet of Things to Cloud Computing and Computer Gamming to Social Media Platforms. The program will prepare graduates to assume responsible positions in business, government, and education at the research, development, and planning levels.
