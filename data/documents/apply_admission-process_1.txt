ADMISSION PROCESS
Admissions at KIET are conducted through a transparent, merit-based, and regulated process, ensuring compliance with Higher Education Commission (HEC) guidelines and relevant accreditation bodies. Admission decisions are based on a combination of academic performance, admission test results, and interview evaluation, where applicable.
Application Submission Process
Prospective applicants are required to submit their applications through the KIET Online Admissions Portal. The application process is designed to be fully online, enabling applicants to apply conveniently from anywhere.
After completing the online application form, candidates must deposit a
non-refundable application processing fee of
PKR 3,000
through the designated HBL bank account or approved online transfer channels.
Applicants are required to upload scanned, attested copies of the following documents:
CNIC or B-Form
Academic transcripts and degrees of all previous qualifications
Two recent passport-size color photographs
Eligible applicants may be required to appear in an admission test and/or interview, depending on the program applied for.
Admission Requirements & Selection Criteria
Admission at KIET is granted strictly on merit, determined through a holistic evaluation of:
Past academic performance
Performance in KIET’s Admission (Aptitude) Test
Interview performance (where applicable)
For postgraduate programs, compliance with HEC testing requirements
Academic Eligibility Criteria
Bachelor’s Degree Programs
KIET offers undergraduate programs in Business, Management Sciences, Computing, and allied disciplines.
Applicants must have passed Intermediate (HSSC) or an equivalent qualification from a recognized board or institution.
For Business & Management Sciences programs:
A minimum of 50% marks in Intermediate or equivalent qualification is required.
For Computing and related programs:
A minimum of 50% marks in Intermediate (Pre-Engineering / Computer Science or equivalent) is required.
Applicants with O-Level / A-Level qualifications must submit an IBCC Equivalence Certificate.
Note: Engineering degree programs are not offered and have been discontinued.
Master’s Degree Programs
Applicants applying for Master’s degree programs must possess a Bachelor’s degree (minimum 16 years of education) from an HEC-recognized institution.
A minimum of 50% marks or Second Division in the Bachelor’s degree is required.
Admission is granted strictly on merit and subject to fulfillment of all academic and institutional requirements.
MS / M.Phil Programs
Applicants seeking admission to MS / M.Phil programs must meet the following criteria:
Completion of 16 years of education (four-year Bachelor’s degree or equivalent) from an HEC-recognized institution
A minimum CGPA of 2.5 (out of 4.0) or equivalent in the Bachelor’s degree
A valid NTS / GAT-General score, meeting HEC minimum passing criteria
Shortlisted candidates may be required to appear for an interview as part of the selection process.
PhD Programs
Admission to PhD programs requires:
An MS / M.Phil or equivalent qualification in a relevant field
A minimum CGPA of 3.0 (out of 4.0) or equivalent
A valid NTS / GAT-Subject score meeting HEC criteria
Admission is subject to availability of suitable research supervision and successful completion of interview and evaluation.
Temporary / Provisional Admission
Applicants who have appeared in their Intermediate, Bachelor’s, or Master’s examinations and are awaiting results may be granted provisional admission, provided all other requirements are fulfilled.
Such applicants must submit a formal undertaking at the time of application. If the applicant fails to meet the minimum eligibility criteria after the declaration of results, the admission shall be cancelled immediately, and any fee refund shall be governed by the HEC Fee Refund Policy.
Admission Test (Aptitude Test)
The KIET Admission Test is designed to assess a candidate’s analytical ability, aptitude, and academic preparedness.
The test duration is approximately two hours
It consists of MCQs and a written/essay component
MCQs assess:
English comprehension, grammar, and vocabulary
Mathematics and quantitative reasoning
IQ and general knowledge
The test structure for undergraduate programs is aligned with internationally recognized aptitude assessment frameworks.
Interview
Candidates who successfully clear the admission test may be invited for an interview prior to final selection.
Interviews are conducted in panel format and aim to assess the candidate’s:
Communication skills
Subject understanding
Personality, confidence, and attitude
General awareness and motivation
Merit List & Final Selection
Final selection is made strictly on merit, taking into account:
Academic record
Admission test performance
Interview evaluation (where applicable)
Selected candidates are informed through the admissions portal, email, and SMS, and are issued formal selection letters.
Fee Payment & Registration
Selected candidates must deposit the prescribed fees within the deadline mentioned in the offer letter. Failure to do so may result in cancellation of the admission offer and allocation of the seat to the next eligible candidate.
Deferment of Admission
Students who are granted admission and wish to defer their enrollment for one semester may do so by submitting a written request to the Admissions Office.
Fee transfer to the subsequent semester is allowed only if the request is submitted within one week of the commencement of the semester. Requests received after this period shall not be entertained.
Remedial Programs
Students who pass the admission test but are found academically weak in English or Mathematics may be required to undertake remedial courses.
These programs are designed to strengthen foundational skills and ensure academic readiness for degree programs at KIET.
Important Notes
KIET reserves the right to revise admission schedules, eligibility criteria, and policies in accordance with HEC, accreditation bodies, and institutional requirements. Applicants are advised to regularly visit the official website for updates.
