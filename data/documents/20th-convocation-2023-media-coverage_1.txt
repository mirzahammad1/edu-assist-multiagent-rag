20th convocation ceremony
- Karachi Institute of
Economics & Technology

EP. Report

KARACHI Karachi
Institute of Economics &
Technology (KIET) hold its
20th Convocation
Ceremony on Wednesday,
15 March, 2023 at KIET
Main Campus, Pakistan Air
Force Airmen Academy,
Korangi Creek, Karachi:

A total of Nine Hundred
& Thirty-four graduates
were conferred degrees in
the disciplines. of
Management Sciences,

Computer Sciences, Arts,
Humanities & Sciences and
Engineering, The College
of Management Sciences
awarded 429 degrees. The

College of Computing &
Information Sciences
awarded 363 degrees. 80
degrees were awarded to
the graduates of College of
Engineering and the
College of Arts,
Humanities & Sciences
awarded 42 degrees to its
graduates. Furthermore, 20
degrees were awarded to
the MS/M.Phil and PhD
scholars.

Honorable Air Marshal
(Retd.) Mr. Mirza Zafar
Hussain, Chancellor KIET,
was the Chief Guest at the
occasion who awarded
Gold and Silver Medals to
the distinguished students.
The Chief Guest empha-

sized the importance of
higher education and the
significance of research and
development for — the
progress of the country. Air
Vice Marshal Hussain
Ahmed Siddiqui, President
Air War College Institute
was the guest of honor at
the ceremony.

In his welcome address,
President of KIET, Air Vice
Marshal (Retd.) Usaid ur
Rehman Usmani_ praised
the efforts of the students
and faculty while empha-
sizing the role of education
and modern research in the
development of Pakistan.
He thanked the Chief Guest
for his gracious presence.
