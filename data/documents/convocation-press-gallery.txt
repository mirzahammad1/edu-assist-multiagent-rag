Convocation Press Gallery
The Convocation Ceremony at KIET marks a proud and defining milestone in the academic journey of our graduates. This momentous occasion celebrates years of dedication, perseverance, and achievement, as students step forward to embrace new opportunities and professional excellence.
Our Press Gallery captures the essence of this prestigious event through a collection of memorable highlights, featuring distinguished guests, faculty members, and graduating students. From inspiring speeches to the conferral of degrees, each moment reflects the spirit of success and the commitment of KIET to academic excellence.
