2nd IQC Meeting Summary
The 2nd meeting of the Institutional Quality Committee (IQC) was conducted to review institutional compliance with Higher Education Commission (HEC) quality assurance frameworks. The committee evaluated the implementation status of previously approved action plans and assessed departmental performance based on defined quality indicators. Key discussions focused on strengthening internal quality assurance systems, standardization of academic and administrative documentation, enhancement of teaching–learning processes, and alignment of institutional practices with HEC guidelines and accreditation requirements. The meeting concluded with the formulation of specific recommendations, assignment of responsibilities, and approval of timelines to ensure systematic monitoring and continuous quality improvement.
2nd IQC Meeting – KIET
The 2nd Institutional Quality Circle (IQC) meeting was held to review ongoing quality enhancement initiatives at KIET. Key discussions focused on:
Completion of Action Points
from the previous IQC meeting.
Review of Academic Quality Processes
, including PREE and RIPE documentation.
Compliance Status
of various academic and administrative standards.
Future Plans
for quality enhancement, aligning with HEC PSG-2023 requirements.
The meeting reinforced KIET’s commitment to continuous quality improvement and institutional excellence through structured monitoring, documented observations, and strategic planning.
