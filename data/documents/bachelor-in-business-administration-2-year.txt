Bachelor in Business Administration 2 Year
BACHELOR IN BUSINESS ADMINISTRATION 2 YEARS
Karachi Institute of Economics and Technology (KIET) develops graduates who emerge as leaders in industry and academia by integrating knowledge with technical and managerial skills for the benefit of society and the environment. The
two-year BBA program
aims to produce well-rounded graduates with broad vision, ethical values, and balanced personalities. Alongside core and elective business courses, students complete general education courses in humanities, social sciences, religion, ethics, language, and communication, enhancing reasoning, social awareness, and personal development. By combining disciplinary expertise, practical skills, and ethical orientation, KIET prepares graduates to contribute effectively to industry, academia, and society while adapting to technological and social change.
BA, B.Com, BSC With minimum 2nd Division or Equivalent
