Associate Degree in Digital Marketing
ASSOCIATE DEGREE IN DIGITAL MARKETING
Karachi Institute of Economics and Technology (KIET) offers a
two-year Associate Degree in Digital Marketing
designed to equip graduates with the skills and knowledge needed to thrive in today’s dynamic digital economy. The program combines hands-on training in social media, SEO, content creation, analytics, and e-commerce with courses in communication, ethics, and critical thinking, ensuring well-rounded professional development. Graduates emerge ready to drive digital marketing strategies, enhance brand visibility, and deliver measurable results for businesses, startups, and organizations, while maintaining ethical practices and adapting to rapidly evolving technological and market trends.
