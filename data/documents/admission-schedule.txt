ADMISSION SCHEDULE
The admission process at
Karachi Institute of Economics and Technology (KIET)
follows a structured,
transparent, and merit-based timeline. Admission announcements are published through leading newspapers,
official social media platforms, and the KIET website. These announcements provide complete details regarding
available degree programs, eligibility criteria, and the application procedure.
Applicants are required to submit their admission application online through the designated admission portal.
Original academic documents are verified at the time of application submission. KIET follows a
semester-based academic system comprising
Spring
and
Fall
semesters.
Important Notice
General Instructions for Admission Test
The last date for receipt of duly completed application forms is
February 28, 2026
Applicants must carefully follow all instructions related to the Admission Test.
The
Admit Card
will be issued only after submission of the completed application form along
with attested photocopies of academic documents and CNIC/Form-B.
Original academic documents must be presented for verification at the time of application submission.
The Admit Card is mandatory for appearing in the Admission Test. Candidates without an Admit Card
will not be allowed to appear in the test.
A sample test paper is available for reference.
Candidates applying for
MS/MPhil
programs are required to submit valid
NTS-GAT
results along with their application.
Final selection will be based on Admission Test performance and previous academic record.
