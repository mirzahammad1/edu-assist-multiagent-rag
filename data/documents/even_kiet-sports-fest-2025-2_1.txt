KIET Sports Fest 2025
The spirit of competition, teamwork, and excellence comes alive at KIET Sports Fest 2025.
This celebration of sportsmanship brings together athletes, students, and teams to compete, connect, and create unforgettable memories. From high-energy indoor games to thrilling cricket matches, the fest reflects discipline, perseverance, and unity beyond the field.
Indoor Games: 23–24 December 2025
Cricket Matches: 30–31 December 2025
Cricket Venue: Asghar Ali Shah Cricket Stadium, North Nazimabad
Let us unite under the banner of sportsmanship and cheer for every effort, every victory, and every moment of determination.
