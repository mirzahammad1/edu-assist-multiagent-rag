GenZ KIETPreneur
GenZ KIETPreneur: Entrepreneurship Exhibition
The College of Management Sciences, KIET organized GenZ KIETPreneur on 5th January 2026 at KIET Shahrah-e-Faisal. The event showcased innovative, student-led business ideas, promoting entrepreneurial thinking among students.
The exhibition was evaluated by distinguished guests Dr. Nida Masroor and Mr. Muhammad Faizan Dilshad, who provided valuable insights and feedback.
An inspiring platform where creativity met entrepreneurship!
