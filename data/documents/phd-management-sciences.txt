PhD-MANAGEMENT SCIENCES
The PhD Program in Management Sciences is designed to develop advanced scholars and researchers capable of addressing complex managerial and socio-economic challenges through rigorous research and critical inquiry. Aligned with the academic and technological orientation of the
Karachi Institute of Economics and Technology (KIET)
, the program promotes intellectual independence, research innovation, interdisciplinary collaboration, and the application of modern analytical tools. Emphasizing industry relevance, ethical responsibility, and sustainable development, it prepares graduates to contribute meaningfully to academia, policy-making, industry, and society at national and global levels.
