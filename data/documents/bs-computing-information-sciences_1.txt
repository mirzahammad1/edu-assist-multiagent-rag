BS Computing & Information Sciences
The Bachelor of Science in Computing and Information Science (BSCIS) is a four-year undergraduate degree program designed to provide students with a strong foundation in computing principles, software development, data science, information systems, and emerging technologies.
The program equips graduates with analytical, technical, and problem-solving skills required in modern computing industries, research, and entrepreneurship.
The curriculum integrates theoretical knowledge, practical laboratory work, industry exposure, and a final year capstone project, enabling students to become competent computing professionals.
