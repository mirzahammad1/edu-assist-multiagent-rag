BS 2.5 Year
Bachelor of Science 2.5 Year (BS 2.5)
The BS Computer Science 2.5 years program is designed for students already having 14 years education i.e. BA, BSc, B.Com and they want to switch their career to Computer Sciences and associated disciplines. After successful completion of the program, the students will get 16 years degree in Computer Science. The program covers all new dimensions of Information Systems and prepare graduates for international market.
