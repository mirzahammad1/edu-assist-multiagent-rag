Associate Degree Program in Enterprise Resource Planning
Associate Degree in ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) is a two-year undergraduate program designed to provide students with practical knowledge of enterprise systems and business process management. The program introduces core concepts of accounting, supply chain, human resource management, and information systems while emphasizing hands-on training with ERP software tools. Students develop technical and organizational skills required to support, operate, and manage integrated business solutions in modern organizations. Graduates are prepared for entry-level roles in ERP support, business operations, and administrative or technical positions across various industries.
