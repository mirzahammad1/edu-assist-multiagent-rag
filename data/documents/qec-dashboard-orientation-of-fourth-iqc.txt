Orientation of FOURTH IQC 2024-25
4th IQC:
The 4th IQC highlighted KIET’s leadership in academic quality, with
Prof. Dr. Sajida Parveen
presenting detailed
PREE and RIPE compliance
, HEC-aligned reporting, and actionable strategies for operational, institutional, and resource optimization, reinforcing KIET’s model for
evidence-based, sustainable quality enhancement
The
4th Institutional Quality Circle (IQC) meeting
at KIET, chaired by
Prof. Dr. Muhammad Khalid Khan, President KIET
, convened statutory officers, deans, and external reviewers, including former SHEC Director Mr. Saleemuddin, to rigorously assess the institution’s quality enhancement progress.
Prof. Dr. Sajida Parveen, Director QEC
, presented a detailed report on the implementation of the
first full academic quality cycle
, demonstrating significant advances in
PREE (Program Review and Evaluation Exercise)
and
RIPE (Reporting, Implementation, and Performance Evaluation) compliance
, with systematic
PDCA-based corrective actions
ensuring measurable improvements. The meeting included a thorough review of all academic units’ documentation, evaluating compliance against RIPE standards, identifying gaps, and mapping progress directly to HEC scorecard indicators for teaching, learning, research, and program effectiveness. Discussions focused on operational, institutional, and resource-related challenges, including data management, faculty engagement, and infrastructure optimization, while external reviewers provided technical recommendations to strengthen evidence-based decision-making and quality reporting. The Chairperson recognized Prof. Dr. Sajida Parveen’s pivotal role in leading these initiatives, underscoring her leadership in driving continuous quality improvement, ensuring HEC-aligned documentation, and establishing a robust foundation for future IQC cycles.
