MoU with Salaam Takaful
Karachi Institute of Economics and Technology (KIET) has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Salaam Takaful (Life & Savings), marking a step toward strengthening collaboration between academia and industry.
This partnership aims to create meaningful opportunities for students and faculty, encourage knowledge sharing, and support professional development through practical industry engagement. Together, both institutions look forward to contributing toward a more connected and sustainable future.
