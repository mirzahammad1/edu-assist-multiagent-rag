Convocation 2026
A proud chapter closes as a brighter one quietly opens. The 23rd Convocation 2026 of KIET, held at the Expo Centre, marked a remarkable celebration of achievement, perseverance, and new beginnings.
Congratulations to our graduates for transforming years of dedication into a moment that will echo for years to come. Surrounded by proud families, esteemed faculty, and distinguished guests, the ceremony reflected the true spirit of excellence and accomplishment.
As our graduates step forward into the professional world, they carry with them the knowledge, confidence, and values nurtured at KIET. This milestone is not just an end, but the beginning of a journey filled with opportunities, growth, and success.
We celebrate your achievements and wish you a future filled with purpose and distinction..
