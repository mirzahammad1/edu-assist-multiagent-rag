KIET Institutional Quality Circle (IQC) Highlights
KIET’s journey toward continuous academic quality improvement has been exemplary under the leadership of Prof. Dr. Sajida Parveen, Director QEC. The 1st IQC launched the institution’s first full academic quality cycle, establishing the framework for PREE and RIPE implementation, faculty engagement, and HEC-compliant practices. In the 2nd IQC, measurable progress in academic programs and compliance with RIPE standards was presented, demonstrating evidence-based reporting and operational efficiency aligned with HEC scorecard indicators. The 3rd IQC marked a milestone with a thorough review of program outcomes, PDCA-based corrective actions, and documentation, showcasing KIET’s success in institutionalizing continuous quality improvement. Finally, the 4th IQC reinforced KIET’s leadership in quality enhancement, with detailed presentations on PREE and RIPE compliance, HEC-aligned reporting, and actionable strategies for optimizing institutional, operational, and resource processes, establishing KIET as a model for sustainable, evidence-driven quality excellence
