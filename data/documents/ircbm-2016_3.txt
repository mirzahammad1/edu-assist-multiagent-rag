Aims and Scope
The “1st National Conference on business management (NRCBM-16)” is scheduled at Karachi institute of economics and technology, Karachi, Pakistan,6
March, 2016. The theme of the conference is “Exchange of Ideas and Knowledge”. The conference will provide a unique forum to the participants to interact with experts of academia and corporate world which will enhance their knowledge and expertise.
Format
Scholars will present their manuscripts through oral presentation. Selected papers will be considered for publication in Market Forces (ISSN-1816-8434), Journal of Management Sciences. Market Forces gives an honorarium to the first authors whose papers are published in the journal.  .
Venue
The Venue for the conference is PAF-Karachi Institute Of Economics And Technology (KIET), City Campus  off Shahra-e Faisal, Karachi, Sindh, Pakistan.
